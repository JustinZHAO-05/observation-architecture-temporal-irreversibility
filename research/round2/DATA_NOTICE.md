# Data attribution and reuse

The neural-recording source is:

Gerwin Schalk (2009). **EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Dataset**, version 1.0.0. PhysioNet. DOI: 10.13026/C28G6P. https://physionet.org/content/eegmmidb/1.0.0/

The source dataset is distributed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0. Attribution is retained for these derived analysis arrays. The included first-round arrays are numerical derivatives, not raw EDF signals. Original sample-level recordings are available at the versioned source. Do not describe the present follow-up as a newly collected dataset.

Additional requested source citations:

G. Schalk, D. J. McFarland, T. Hinterberger, N. Birbaumer and J. R. Wolpaw. **BCI2000: A General-Purpose Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) System.** IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering 51(6), 1034–1043 (2004).

T. Pollard, B. E. Moody, L.-w. Lehman, B. Gow, C. Fernandes, C. Xie, A. Johnson, R. G. Mark and T. Heldt. **PhysioNet as a global platform for biomedical research.** Nature Health 1, 792–795 (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s44360-026-00096-z.

The simulation tables and code were generated in this research session. They contain no private human records. Participant identifiers in the derived EEG files are the public pseudonymous dataset identifiers.

The bundle contains no third-party full-text papers and no font files. Literature is supplied by bibliographic references and primary-source links.
