## Scope of the novelty statement

The specific representation question fixes H(omega)=[I,exp(-i omega)I], requires a real even positive-definite latent spectrum, and bounds its dimension by 2d. Classical spectral factorization instead allows a target-dependent matrix filter on white innovations. The white-latent MA example is a standard realization special case and is credited accordingly. The new finite-order proof preserves the fixed H while restricting the latent covariance support to be finite. The broadband resource theorem is for the rotational family with fixed gains and unrestricted causal realization order. The independent calibration theorem is conditional on a quantitative dependence class, but does not need the unknown spectrum's normalized shape.

This is a bounded primary-source comparison. Final scholarly priority and validity remain open to normal external peer review; the manuscript avoids an unqualified “first” claim.
